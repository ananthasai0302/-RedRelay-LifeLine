# RedRelay – LifeLine

## Short Project Explanation

RedRelay – LifeLine is an emergency blood request and donor coordination website. It helps patients find compatible blood donors nearby during emergencies. Users can post blood requests, and eligible donors can respond. The platform tracks requests from posting to fulfilment while protecting donor privacy.

## Important Note

RedRelay is a coordination platform only. The hospital blood bank and medical professionals must verify donor eligibility, blood type, screening, and crossmatching before any donation.
